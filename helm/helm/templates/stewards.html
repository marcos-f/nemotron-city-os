federation stewards

What this role is, and what it is not

federation-steward is a voting franchise. A holder may vote in another dataset's break-glass quorum — and that is the whole of it. It confers no authority over any dataset: no grants, no delegations, no approvals. A steward who is a viewer in this console is still a viewer.

warrant needs it because break-glass requires two votes from admins of other datasets across at least two distinct datasets, so one person who administers six datasets is one vote, not six. On a small or single-dataset federation no such quorum can form, and the stewards are the named people who may supply one.

It is assigned here, by a helm admin, and every change is written to the ledger. A dataset admin cannot grant it — someone who could hand out the franchise could manufacture the quorum that exists to check them.
